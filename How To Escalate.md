# How to Escalate to Different Teams

Different departments handle different types of cases. Sometimes you'll need to escalate to another team. This is either because a ticket was misrouted or the customer's concern developed to fall outside your scope. This article explains how to escalate to the right department.

Support typically uses a mix of the following:
<ul>
<li>Macros: Pre-written responses.</li>
<li>Reusable Workflows: Move ticket to specific queues.</li>
<li>Back-office tickets: Communicate internally with escalated teams.</li>
</ul>
<p class="note--message">
Macros are specific to replies and internal notes. Set the message type to match the step (Reply or Internal Note) before applying the Macro.</p>
